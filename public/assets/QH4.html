Rightworks Cloud for QuickBooks Hosting FAQs

Rightworks Cloud Hosting is an intelligent cloud platform that unifies all of your essential accounting, tax and associated business applications, including QuickBooks® Online, QuickBooks Desktop and QuickBooks Enterprise into one cloud work environment, offering users the flexibility of secure anytime, anywhere access. Rightworks Cloud Hosting enriches client engagement and business performance with actionable business and client insights, user management, backup, disaster recovery, unmatched industry expertise and 24/7 award-winning support.

In addition to streamlining your workflow and giving your team a single, reliable place to work every day, Rightworks Cloud Hosting provides a scalable, cost-effective cybersecurity solution that offers enterprise-level protection to operations of any size. This important additional layer of security to how you access your web-based applications, including QuickBooks Online, ensures your business data remains safe and secure.

Rightworks Cloud Hosting supports various versions, including QuickBooks Online, Pro, Premier and Enterprise, allowing businesses to use the version that best fits their needs, alongside their other critical business applications, whether web-based or desktop. This flexibility ensures that, regardless of the version, users can enjoy cloud access, collaboration and enhanced security features.

QuickBooks Online is a cloud-based accounting solution that lets users access their data from any internet-connected device and includes automated updates and integrations. QuickBooks hosted refers to the desktop versions of QuickBooks hosted in the cloud, allowing remote access and collaboration while retaining the complete desktop experience.

Whether you use QuickBooks Online or QuickBooks Desktop applications, Rightworks Cloud Hosting offers enterprise-grade security features, including advanced data encryption technologies and multi-layered security protocols to protect sensitive information. Rightworks implements regular automated backups, ensuring data recovery in case of unexpected events. Additionally, robust firewall protections and intrusion detection systems work to prevent unauthorized access. With these comprehensive security measures, Rightworks Cloud Hosting offers a reliable and safe environment for your critical business and financial data, not only for QuickBooks, but across all the applications you use every day.
